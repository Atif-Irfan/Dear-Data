Evaluation of the suitability of the data query:

1. Is it feasible?

Yes this data query can be researched and represented with the given time and resources.
It is free and remote.

2. Is it original?

Yes, however the idea comes from a similar foundation to week 27 and partially from week 41
in the "Dear Data" project by Giorgia Lupi and Stefanie Posavec.

The nature of my data query explores my: psychological habits, triggers
and preferences. This makes my approach analytical and predictable in comparison to
theirs where the data is purely tracked retrospectively.

3. Is it meaningful?

Yes, it provides a deep understanding of how I approach my own personalised YouTube algorithm.

4. Does it maintain anonymity?

Yes, the content outlined and the various behavioural decisions logged keep the query vague
enough that it cannot be found out exactly what channels I watch.

5. Is it numerous?

It WILL be once all data is recorded. My activity on youtube is high so, I got more than enough
data to work with.

Planning for Data Gathering and Representation:

1. How will I source this data?

This will be explained more in detail in the text file where the data sourcing and records will be stored.
The main idea is taking the number of videos YouTube has indexed into my watch history 'V', and generating
a random number X that will be the starting position of an indexed video (counting from top to bottom) such
that 0 ≤ X ≤ V.

2. How will I record this data?

I will be using excell to record the data points of the videos logged through the proposed sourcing
strategy.

3. What programming language will I use?

This data will be represented through processing as required by the Creative Coding module.
